This jutsu, a hallmark of the Hōzuki clan, allows the user to liquefy any part of their body at will, from the hair on their head to the muscles in their arms. Gengetsu Hōzuki liquefies into a mixture of oil and water,Naruto chapter 556, page 2 but it's not clear if this same mixture is used by other members. When the user is struck by a physical attack, the impacted area becomes liquid, causing the attack to pass through them and leave them unharmed. The user then solidifies back into their original form. This technique is generally autonomous, allowing for members to liquefy without conscious thought.

In addition to defence, Hōzuki can convert their body into liquid for any number of purposes, such as infiltrating buildings or sneaking up on targets; the anime depicts users camouflaging themselves for the latter purpose.Boruto episode 29 They can pump water into their muscles to increase their strength,Naruto chapter 348, page 15 shoot water from their fingers as an attack,Naruto chapter 556, page 9 or use their bodies to douse targets. Despite the versatility of their liquid states, it renders Hōzuki very vulnerable to Lightning Release.Naruto chapter 463, page 10 If an attack overwhelms a user and they pass out, their unconscious body will take on a jelly-like state.Naruto chapter 414, page 15 If a Hōzuki is captured and placed inside a sealed container, they will be unable to move or escape.
