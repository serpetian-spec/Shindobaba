The user wraps their body in a layer of lightning chakra to increase their physical parameters and , with more lightning causing greater increases. As users coat more lightning chakra around themselves, the more their hair sticks up.Naruto chapter 463, page 8 The lightning augments the strength of the user's taijutsu, creating what is called nintaijutsu.Kai no Sho, page 120 The lightning also defends the user from most attacks, deflecting them away on contact;Naruto chapter 462, pages 16-17 the Fourth Raikage is impressed when Chidori pierces through to him, however slightly.Naruto chapter 463, pages 2-3

The lightning chakra stimulates the user's nervous system, enhancing their speed and their reaction time.Naruto chapter 462, page 3 The user's reflexes are heightened enough to perform a , dodging attacks as fast as Amaterasu.Naruto chapter 463, pages 13-15 The user is so fast that not even the Sharingan can track their movements; only Minato Namikaze's Flying Thunder God Technique and Naruto Uzumaki's Nine-Tails Chakra Mode have been shown to be faster.Naruto chapter 542, page 14Naruto chapter 544, pages 11-13
